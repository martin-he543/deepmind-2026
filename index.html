RetroSwitch-XGB: Predicting Active Viral Switches from DNA Sequence & Chromatin

We trained a lightweight XGBoost + Random Forest model on GRCh38 FASTA sequence features (TF binding motifs, CpG density, 4-mer complexity, viral divergence, TSS distance) and local chromatin to predict AlphaGenome virtual knockout impact.
