1. Tissue-Specific Co-option

          
LTR17 / ERVW-1 and MER21A dominate in Placenta, LTR7 (HERV-H) is strictly active in Embryonic Stem Cells, and THE1B / MER41B wire Monocyte/Immune enhancers.

        

        

          
2. Most Solo-LTRs Are Dormant Fossils

          
Across 412 tested LTRs within 50 kb of a TSS, 85.2% show knockout effects indistinguishable from random non-repeat deletions (|Z| < 2). The switches that survive are extreme outliers.

        

        

          
3. Why Controls Were Essential

          
Raw ΔRNA-seq alone has a 19% false-positive rate due to interval shift artifacts. Normalizing against matched Alu/L1 and random deletions isolates true viral regulatory switches.
